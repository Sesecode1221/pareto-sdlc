5. Business Logic Documentation

5.1 Fuel Reconciliation & Shrinkage Engine

Mathematical Balance: Variance = Invoiced − (Δ Dip + Metered Burn)

≤ 2.5%
RECONCILED (Normal tolerance)
2.5% – 5.0%
SHRINKAGE_WARNING (Amber)
> 5.0%
CRITICAL_THEFT_ALERT (Red) → COO & Security

5.2 Water Mass Balance & Night-Flow Watchdog

Mass Balance: Unaccounted = Bulk IN − (Tenants + Restrooms + Cooling Towers + Landscape + Borehole/Rainwater)

Night-Flow Rules (01:00–04:00):

  • Baseline allowance: 3.0 kL/hr
  • > 3.0 kL/hr → ELEVATED_NOCTURNAL_FLOW
  • > 4.5 kL/hr sustained 45 min → CRITICAL_LEAK_CONFIRMED + WhatsApp dispatch

5.3 Solar PV String Telemetry & BESS Dispatch Logic

String Health Grading: GREEN (Optimal) · YELLOW (Soiled / Diode) · RED (Blown fuse / Offline)

BESS Modes: TOU Arbitrage (default) · Peak Demand Shaving (>88% NMD) · Solar Absorption (reverse-power prevention) · Emergency Backup (SoC ≥ 85% at Stage 4+)

5.4 IAQ & BMS Setpoint Override Audit

IAQ thresholds: CO₂ >1000 ppm (Warning), >1200 ppm (damper boost); PM2.5 >25 (HEPA); TVOC >500 ppb (alert).

Override drift: manual_override > 4.0 hrs → wasted cost = delta kW × hours × R2.45 → 1-click revert via BACnet priority relinquish.

5.5 IPMVP Option C Statistical Normalization

Multivariate regression with HDD/CDD, footfall, load-shedding suppression. R² = 0.942, CV(RMSE) = 4.8%. Section 12L deduction = verified kWh × R0.95.

5.6 Data Governance & POPIA Privacy

Tenant PII salted & SHA-256 encrypted. Sovereign hosting, zero offshore transmission.
